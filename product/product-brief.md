# Product Brief — Muse 4 Kids

## One-line concept

Muse 4 Kids is a parent-held account experience where each child participates through a kid profile, with an optional paid family chat moderated by Muse.

## Problem framing

Families need a child-appropriate way to participate in a shared conversation while keeping the parent as the account holder and introducing a clear safety layer. The family chat should preserve the value of the existing relay concept while presenting a PG experience.

## Product principles

1. **Parent-held account:** billing, account ownership, and primary controls belong to the parent.
2. **Kid profile simplicity:** a child participates through a profile inside the family account.
3. **Safety by design:** moderation is part of the family-chat experience, not an afterthought.
4. **Clear scope:** integrations such as WhatsApp and SMS remain test-later and are not advertised as launch capabilities.
5. **Consistent identity:** the repo, product, and visual identity use “Muse 4 Kids.”

## Initial scope

- Parent account creation and sign-in.
- Creation and management of one or more kid profiles, subject to final limits.
- A family-chat upsell with Muse moderation.
- Parent visibility into the family-chat experience and safety controls, subject to final UX and policy decisions.
- Basic reporting, moderation events, and support workflows.

## Explicitly out of current advertised scope

- WhatsApp integration.
- SMS integration.
- Any other external messaging integration.
- Independent kid-owned accounts.
- Claims about compliance, therapeutic outcomes, or guaranteed safety.

## Open decisions

- Pricing and plan names.
- Whether the family chat is one parent plus one or more kid profiles, or supports additional approved adults.
- Exact meaning of “PG” in product policy and enforcement.
- Human escalation and response-time expectations.
- Data retention, deletion, export, and regional availability.
- Age bands, parental consent flow, and eligibility requirements.
