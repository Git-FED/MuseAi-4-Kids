# Roadmap — Muse 4 Kids

## Phase 0: Alignment and safety definition

- Confirm the product name and FED-EDU * 4 KIDS label usage.
- Confirm parent-account and kid-profile permissions.
- Define the PG moderation policy and escalation model.
- Resolve consent, age-band, privacy, retention, and regional launch questions.
- Confirm paid upsell packaging and billing requirements.

## Phase 1: Core family-account foundation

- Build parent account flows.
- Build kid-profile creation and management.
- Add account/profile permission boundaries.
- Establish analytics, support, and audit foundations.

## Phase 2: Moderated family chat

- Implement the paid entitlement.
- Implement the family chat experience.
- Add Muse moderation, moderation outcomes, and safe failure behavior.
- Add parent controls and approved support/reporting flows.
- Run safety, accessibility, and usability testing.

## Phase 3: Controlled launch

- Launch to a limited cohort after required approvals.
- Monitor moderation quality, false positives/negatives, support volume, and family experience.
- Iterate policy and product behavior with versioned change records.

## Phase 4: Integration testing (not advertised yet)

- Evaluate WhatsApp, SMS, and other integrations only as test-later work.
- Do not market or promise these integrations until product, safety, privacy, and technical approvals are complete.
