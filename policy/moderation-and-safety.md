# Initial Moderation and Safety Posture — Muse 4 Kids

## Purpose

The paid family chat is intended to be a PG version of the relay already in use, with Muse moderating the conversation. This document is a starting posture for product and safety review, not a final child-safety policy.

## Core behavior

Muse should help keep the conversation age-appropriate, respectful, and useful. When content is unsafe or outside the PG boundary, Muse should avoid amplifying it, explain the boundary in simple language, and offer a safer direction where appropriate.

## Proposed outcomes

- **Allow:** Content is within the current policy.
- **Gentle redirect:** Content is ambiguous, mildly inappropriate, or better answered in a safer form.
- **Warn:** A participant is approaching a boundary and needs a clear reminder.
- **Block:** Content should not be delivered in the family chat.
- **Escalate:** The event requires parent/support or safety review under final policy.

## Content areas requiring explicit policy definitions

- Sexual content and sexual grooming.
- Graphic violence or instructions for harm.
- Self-harm or suicide-related content.
- Abuse, exploitation, or requests for secrecy from a parent/guardian.
- Hate, harassment, bullying, or threats.
- Dangerous activities, weapons, drugs, and illegal activity.
- Personal data sharing and attempts to move the conversation to an unapproved channel.
- Commercial solicitation or manipulative behavior.

## Parent role

The parent is the account holder and should receive clear information about the family-chat controls, limitations, and safety behavior. The exact notification and escalation design is TBD and must balance child privacy, parent visibility, and safety obligations.

## Fail-safe behavior

If moderation is unavailable or uncertain, the system should not silently deliver potentially unsafe content. Engineering and safety must select and document the fallback behavior before launch.

## Human review

The product should define when automated moderation is insufficient, how a human reviewer or support team is engaged, what response time is targeted, and how sensitive records are protected.

## Required approvals before launch

- Final PG policy and examples.
- Age bands and eligibility.
- Parental consent and verification approach.
- Escalation and emergency-response procedures.
- Data retention, deletion, and access policy.
- Testing plan, including adversarial and edge-case evaluation.
